# Citation format — KSC

How to cite, with worked examples.

## The four citation modes

### 1. Foundational texts

**Article 162 of the Constitution of Kosovo:**
> *Constitution of the Republic of Kosovo*, art. 162 (Amendment 24, 2015).

**Law 05/L-053:**
> *Law on Specialist Chambers and Specialist Prosecutor's Office*, Law No. 05/L-053 (3 August 2015), art. [N]. ("the Law")

**Rules of Procedure and Evidence:**
> *Rules of Procedure and Evidence before the Kosovo Specialist Chambers*, rule [N] (revision in force at the date of the cited decision).

### 2. Decisions and judgments

**General form:**
> *Specialist Prosecutor v. [Defendant(s)]*, Case No. [KSC case number], [Document Title], [Panel / Chamber], [Date], para. [N].

**Case-number anatomy:**
- **KSC-BC-[YYYY]-[NN]** — "Before Chambers", year of case opening, sequential number. The primary case identifier for trial-level proceedings.
- **KSC-CA-[YYYY]-[NN]** — Court of Appeals
- **KSC-SC-[YYYY]-[NN]** — Supreme Court Chamber
- **KSC-CC-[YYYY]-[NN]** — Constitutional Court Chamber

**Worked examples:**

- *Specialist Prosecutor v. Mustafa*, Case No. KSC-BC-2020-05, Trial Judgment (Trial Panel I), 16 December 2022, para. [N].
- *Specialist Prosecutor v. Mustafa*, Case No. KSC-CA-2022-08, Appeal Judgment (Court of Appeals Panel), 14 December 2023, para. [N].
- *Specialist Prosecutor v. Shala*, Case No. KSC-BC-2020-04, Trial Judgment (Trial Panel I), 16 July 2024, para. [N].
- *Specialist Prosecutor v. Shala*, Case No. KSC-BC-2020-04, Reparation Order (Trial Panel I), 29 November 2024.
- *Specialist Prosecutor v. Shala*, Case No. KSC-CA-2024-09, Appeal Judgment (Court of Appeals Panel), 14 July 2025.
- *Specialist Prosecutor v. Thaçi, Veseli, Selimi and Krasniqi*, Case No. KSC-BC-2020-06 (trial ongoing as of 2026).
- *Specialist Prosecutor v. Gucati and Haradinaj*, Case No. KSC-BC-2020-07, Trial Judgment (Trial Panel I), 18 May 2022.

### 3. Indictments

**Form:**
> *Confirmed Indictment*, *Specialist Prosecutor v. [Defendant(s)]*, Case No. KSC-BC-[YYYY]-[NN], [date of confirmation by Pre-Trial Judge].

For the Thaçi et al. case, the indictment was initially confirmed on **26 October 2020** and amended on 3 September 2021, 29 April 2022, and 30 September 2022. The operative indictment is the lesser-redacted version filed 27 February 2023. Cite the operative version when relevant.

### 4. Filings

**Form:**
> *Specialist Prosecutor v. [Defendant(s)]*, Case No. KSC-BC-[YYYY]-[NN], [Filing title], F[Filing number], [Date].

## Names and spellings

Albanian names retain diacritics: **Thaçi** (not Thaci), **Krasniqi** (not Krasniki), **Veseli**, **Selimi**, **Gucati**, **Haradinaj**. Be precise.

Serbian-side identifications appear in some KSC filings; cite as written in the official document.

## Status of accused

For each case, identify whether the accused is **detained**, **on conditional release**, **acquitted**, **convicted (trial level)**, **convicted (final on appeal)**, or **awaiting judgment**. The KSC's status changes regularly — verify before asserting.

As of early 2026, **Hashim Thaçi has been in pre-trial / trial detention since 5 November 2020**. The Thaçi et al. case is *awaiting judgment*.

## When the citation cannot be completed

If, after verification, you cannot identify the case number, document title, panel, or date, the citation is not yet a citation. Narrow the claim, or tell the user what is missing.
