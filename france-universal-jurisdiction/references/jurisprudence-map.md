# Jurisprudence map — France, universal jurisdiction

## 1. The Rwanda line — basis: the 1996 ICTR-adaptation statute (NOT 689-11)

- **Pascal Simbikangwa** — Cour d'assises de Paris, **14 March 2014**, 25 years
  (participation in genocide + complicity in crimes against humanity, Rwanda
  1994); confirmed on appeal, **Cour d'assises de Bobigny, December 2016**
  (25 years). The **first** trial in France for the Rwandan genocide.
  *(Existence/outcome verified via FIDH, HRW, France 24; the cassation outcome
  is `[verify]`.)*
- The Rwanda line continued with further *cours d'assises* cases (e.g. **Ngenzi
  & Barahira** `[verify dates/outcome]`). 
- **Discipline:** these rest on the **specific 1996 law adapting French law to
  the ICTR** (Loi n° 96-432 `[verify]`), **not** on CPP Art. 689-11. Do not
  describe them as "Art. 689-11 universal jurisdiction."

## 2. Kunti Kamara (Liberia) — basis: 689-2 (torture/CAT) + 689-11

- **Cour d'assises de Paris, 2 November 2022** — **life imprisonment**;
  complicity in crimes against humanity and acts of torture/barbarism, committed
  in Lofa County, Liberia (1993–1994), as a ULIMO commander.
- **Cour d'assises d'appel de Paris, 29 March 2024** — conviction confirmed,
  sentence **30 years**.
- Significance: the **first** French universal-jurisdiction trial **not** linked
  to the Rwandan genocide, with the **accused present** — reportedly the fifth
  such trial in France; first French conviction for atrocities in the Liberian
  civil wars.
- *(Trial and appeal existence/outcomes verified via HRW, Civitas Maxima,
  JusticeInfo. Full judgments not public → content at "existence + official
  account" level.)*

## 3. The Syria cases and the 12 May 2023 double-criminality rulings — basis: 689-11

- **Cour de cassation, assemblée plénière, 12 May 2023** — n° **22-80.057**
  (*Chaban*, former Syrian soldier, complicity in crimes against humanity) and
  n° **22-82.468** (*Nema* / "Islam Alloush", former Jaysh al-Islam spokesperson,
  war crimes): the **double-criminality** condition of Art. 689-11 does **not**
  require an identical legal *qualification* in both systems — only that the
  **underlying acts** be criminalised in both. A landmark broadening of French
  competence over Syria crimes. *(Verified this session; Légifrance
  `JURITEXT000047545821` and `JURITEXT000047545823`.)*
- The underlying trials (Chaban; Nema) and other Syria proceedings: `[verify

| Case / line | Crime | Basis | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rwanda (Simbikangwa, …) | genocide / CAH | **1996 ICTR-adaptation law** | NOT 689-11 |
| Kunti Kamara | CAH + torture | **689-2 (CAT) + 689-11** | accused present |

| Lafarge | complicity in CAH | corporate; `[verify basis]` | corporate liability |

**The recurring error** is to flatten all of these into "France exercised
universal jurisdiction under Art. 689-11." They rest on different bases, with
different conditions (presence vs residence vs the Rwanda statute). State the
basis precisely, and verify it.
