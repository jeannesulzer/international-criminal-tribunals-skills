# Changelog — France Universal Jurisdiction skill

All notable changes to the `france-universal-jurisdiction` skill are documented
in this file. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/)
and the project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [0.1.0-draft] — 2026-06-04

### Added
- Initial **pilot draft** of the France universal-jurisdiction skill — the
  first skill in the suite covering a *national* jurisdiction exercising
  universal/extraterritorial jurisdiction over core international crimes.
- `SKILL.md` — entry point: the verification-first discipline adapted to the
  harder national-court context, the verification gradient, the standard
  workflow, the **four-bucket source hierarchy** (citable authority / analysis
  / context-only / do-not-cite-as-authority), the **jurisdictional-basis trap**
  (689-2 vs 689-11 vs the Rwanda statute vs personality-based jurisdiction),
  citation overview, audit mode, sensitive contexts, and the hard rules.
- `references/foundational-texts.md` — the statutory and treaty bases (CPP
  Arts. 689-1, 689-2, 689-11 and the sub-title Arts. 627–628-10; Code pénal
  Arts. 211-1 ff., 212-1 ff., 461-1 ff.; the Geneva Conventions, CAT, Genocide
  Convention), with the 12 May 2023 Cour de cassation rulings on double
  criminality flagged as live law.
- `references/authoritative-sources.md` — the four-bucket hierarchy in detail,
  with the fallback ladder for non-public assises judgments.
- `references/citation-format.md` — Légifrance article-ID + version discipline;
  Cour de cassation *pourvoi*/ECLI conventions; cour d'assises conventions with
  the anonymisation rule.
- `references/verification-workflow.md` — the adapted ladder, the realistic
  ceiling for assises judgments, and the anonymisation and language rules.
- `references/jurisprudence-map.md` — landmark French cases (Simbikangwa and
  the Rwanda line; Kunti Kamara; the Syria cases and the 12 May 2023 rulings;
  Lafarge), each flagged with its actual jurisdictional basis and verification
  status.
- `examples/example-verification.md` — the Kunti Kamara citation verified end
  to end (trial 2 November 2022 / appeal 29 March 2024).
- `examples/example-audit.md` — auditing a draft that mislabels the
  jurisdictional basis and cites a people's tribunal as authority.

### Status
- **Draft for expert review.** Statutory references were verified against
  Légifrance (via the Legal Data Hunter dataset); landmark-case facts were
  verified against Tier-1/Tier-2 public sources. Items not fully confirmed in
  this pass are marked `[verify]` in the files and must be confirmed by a
  practitioner before public reliance — in particular the precise current
  conditions of Art. 689-11 after the 2023 reform, the exact creation dates and
  competence split of the Paris pôle / PNAT / OCLCH, and the *cassation*
  outcomes of the assises cases.
