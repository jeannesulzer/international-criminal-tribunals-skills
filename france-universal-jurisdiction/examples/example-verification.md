# Example — verifying a French universal-jurisdiction citation end to end

A worked example on the **Kunti Kamara** case, showing the realistic ceiling
when a first-instance *cour d'assises* judgment is not public.

## The request

> "Can you confirm France convicted a former Liberian commander under universal
> jurisdiction, with the outcome and dates, for a memo on UJ in Europe?"

## Step 0 — Identify
- **Forum:** Cour d'assises de Paris (first instance); Cour d'assises d'appel de
  Paris (appeal).
- **Accused:** Kunti Kamara (named in the official/public record — not
  anonymised).
- **Crimes:** complicity in crimes against humanity + acts of torture/barbarism,
  Lofa County, Liberia, 1993–1994.
- **Jurisdictional basis:** torture via CPP **art. 689-2** (CAT) together with
  the **689-11** regime for crimes against humanity — *not* the Rwanda statute,
  *not* "pure 689-11" alone. (Confirm the exact basis against the record.)

## Step 1 — List
- The trial judgment (existence, date, outcome).
- The appeal judgment (existence, date, outcome).
- The statutory bases (689-2; 689-11) at the version in force.

## Step 2 — Verify (ladder)
- **Légifrance** — confirm CPP arts. 689-2 (`LEGIARTI000006577257`) and 689-11
  (`LEGIARTI000048442165`).
- **Cour de cassation** — n/a unless a *pourvoi* is in play.
- **Official / Tier-2 record** — the full assises judgment is **not public**.
  Existence and outcome are confirmed via **Human Rights Watch**, **Civitas
  Maxima**, and **JusticeInfo**:
  - Trial: **Cour d'assises de Paris, 2 November 2022 — life imprisonment.**
  - Appeal: **Cour d'assises d'appel de Paris, 29 March 2024 — 30 years.**
- **Ceiling reached:** *existence + official/Tier-2 account.* The full reasoning
  is not publicly available, so **paragraph-level content is not verified.**

## Step 3 — Draft (with the level stated)

> France has exercised extraterritorial jurisdiction over core international
> crimes committed in the Liberian civil wars. In *Kunti Kamara*, the **Cour
> d'assises de Paris (2 November 2022)** convicted a former ULIMO commander of
> complicity in crimes against humanity and acts of torture/barbarism committed
> in Lofa County (1993–1994), sentencing him to life imprisonment; on appeal,
> the **Cour d'assises d'appel de Paris (29 March 2024)** confirmed the
> conviction and imposed 30 years. It is reported to be the first French
> universal-jurisdiction trial unconnected to the Rwandan genocide with the
> accused present. [Existence and outcomes verified via HRW, Civitas Maxima and
> JusticeInfo; the full assises judgments are not public, so the reasoning is
> not verified at paragraph level. Jurisdictional basis (CPP arts. 689-2 / 689-11)
> to be confirmed against the record.]

## Step 4 — Self-audit
- Jurisdictional basis stated and flagged for confirmation: yes.
- Statute versions checked: yes (Légifrance IDs).
- Verification level stated honestly (not paragraph-level): yes.
- Anonymisation: Kamara is named in the public record — correct to name him.
- Appeal outcome (revised from life to 30 years) reflected: yes.
